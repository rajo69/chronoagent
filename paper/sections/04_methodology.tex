\section{Methodology}
\label{sec:methodology}

\subsection{Behavioral Signals}
\label{sec:methodology:signals}

Let $s_{a,t} \in \mathbb{R}^6$ denote the signal vector for agent $a$
at PR-handling step $t$. The six components are:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Response latency} (wall-clock time around the LLM
  call, ms).
  \item \textbf{Retrieval count} (documents returned from the shared
  memory).
  \item \textbf{Token count} of the LLM input.
  \item \textbf{KL divergence} $\kldiv(q_t \parallel p_0)$ of the
  step's retrieval-embedding distribution against a diagonal Gaussian
  fitted on clean calibration steps.
  \item \textbf{Tool-call frequency} (tool and retrieval calls per
  step).
  \item \textbf{Memory-query entropy} (Shannon entropy of the top-$k$
  similarity scores).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{BOCPD + Chronos Ensemble}
\label{sec:methodology:detector}

We apply a Bayesian Online Change-Point Detector
(BOCPD)~\citep{adams2007bocpd} to the KL divergence stream, using a
Gaussian observation model with a Normal-Inverse-Chi-Squared conjugate
prior. The run-length posterior $p(r_t \mid s_{1:t})$ collapses when a
change point occurs, and the changepoint probability
$p(r_t = 0 \mid s_{1:t})$ is the BOCPD anomaly score.

In parallel, a Chronos (T5-small) forecaster~\citep{chronos} forecasts
the signal from its history (at least 10 steps) and scores how far the
observation falls outside the predicted
$[\hat{q}_{0.1}, \hat{q}_{0.9}]$ range. The ensemble fuses the two
scores into the health score
$h = 1 - \mathrm{clip}(w_{\text{bocpd}}\, s_{\text{bocpd}} + w_{\text{chronos}}\, s_{\text{chronos}}, 0, 1)$
with default weights $0.5$ and $0.5$, and promotes the remaining
weight to $1.0$ when one branch is unavailable.

\subsection{Advance Warning Time}
\label{sec:methodology:awt}

For each run we define Advance Warning Time as
\begin{equation}
  \awt{} = t_{\text{injection}} - t_{\text{first\_flagged}},
\end{equation}
where $t_{\text{injection}}$ is the ground-truth step at which the
poisoning payload was written to memory. $\awt{} > 0$ means the
detector fired before the payload took effect (early warning);
$\awt{} = 0$ means concurrent detection (Pivot~A);
$\awt{} < 0$ means the detector fired after the payload took effect.

\subsection{Health-Weighted Allocation}
\label{sec:methodology:alloc}

The allocation layer is a shallow contract-net
variant~\citep{contractnet}; a Dec-POMDP view of the underlying
coordination problem is given by~\citet{decpomdp}. Agent $a$'s bid
for task $j$ is
\begin{equation}
  b_{a,j} = \text{cap}(a, j) \cdot h_a,
\end{equation}
where $\text{cap}(a, j) \in (0, 1]$ is a static proficiency matrix
(1.0 for the designated specialist) and $h_a \in [0, 1]$ is the agent's
health score. The highest bid at or above a threshold (default 0.25)
wins the task; when no bid clears it, the task escalates to a human
reviewer. An agent whose health score drops to zero bids zero and
receives no new work until it recovers.
